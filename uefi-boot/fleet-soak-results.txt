     committed scripts.

     "ok" = count of "verify OK" accumulated before the kill (target >=1000,
     capped at 45s of wall time; a few runs capped early, hence 393-583).
  3. Node A is killed by exact PID (never by process name). "bAlive" = node B
     qemu still alive after the kill. "denied" = "verify DENIED" lines seen in
     the 12s after the kill.
  4. Boundary: "lastOK" = serial line of the last "verify OK",
     "firstDEN" = serial line of the first "verify DENIED". All runs flip on
     the very next verify (firstDEN = lastOK + 1).
  5. RX diagnostics (rd_h/rd_t/next/packets/polls/empty/sat/bad/max_drain/
     bad_status/bad_len) recorded before and after the kill from node B's log.
